How It Works

Step 1: Launch and Drag

Open Mp3 Player. Drag MP3s, AACs, or any supported files from Finder directly into the window.

Step 2: Play Instantly

Hit play. Audio streams from disk—no copies, no iTunes involvement. Create playlists on the fly.

Step 3: Control Effortlessly

Use menu bar controls, global shortcuts, or the clean interface. Adjust volume, seek, repeat as needed.

Step 4: Enjoy Pure Audio

Close when done. Files stay exactly where they are. Reopen anytime for immediate access.
